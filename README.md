I've worked in software engineering and consulting since 2012. At Consid I lead the Camunda topic and manage a team of five consultants. Before that I spent twelve years at Novatec. I started in Java backends, CI/CD and configuration management and joined Novatec's BPM guild in 2014. From 2022 I was a principal engineer and people lead for one of the two BPM teams, working on Camunda and Kafka architectures for logistics and e-commerce.

I like to stay hands-on, from the BPMN model and the Java code down to the Helm chart and the on-call runbook. My diploma thesis at Ulm University was a small data-centric process engine, so I've been building things that move processes forward for a while.

## 🔭 What I'm Working On

- **Camunda 8 on sovereign cloud.** I designed and built Consid's Camunda 8 Self-Managed platform on [STACKIT](https://stackit.com), the Schwarz Group's European cloud. It's built entirely as code with Terraform, ArgoCD and Entra ID, it's hardened, and it's sized from a load test against production. The whole story is in the seven-part [Camunda On Sovereign Cloud](https://blog.itsmestefanjay.dev/p/owning-your-process-automation-the-49a) series.
- **Agentic orchestration.** AI agents inside BPMN, with tools, humans in the loop and models you can actually run in Europe.
- **Connectors.** Custom connectors, secret providers and the runtime underneath them.
